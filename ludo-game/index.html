Game Settings

Ludo Rules

  • Starting: Roll a 6 to release a token from your Home Yard onto the starting square.
  • Movement: Roll the dice to move active tokens clockwise around the board.
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their Yard! (Except on Safe / Star squares).
  • Safe Squares: Starting tiles and Star tiles are safe from capture.
  • Extra Turns: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or bringing a token to the Finish grants an extra roll.
  • Winning: The first team to get all 4 tokens into the central Finish area wins!
