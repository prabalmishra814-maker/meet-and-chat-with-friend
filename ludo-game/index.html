Ludo Rules

  • Starting: Roll a 6 to release a token from your Home Yard.
  • Movement: Move tokens clockwise according to dice rolls.
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their Yard!
  • Safe Squares: Star tiles and Starting tiles are safe from capture.
  • Extra Turns: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or reaching Home grants an extra roll.
  • Winning: Bring all 4 tokens into the central Finish area to win!
